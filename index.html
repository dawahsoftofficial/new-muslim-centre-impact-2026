01 / CEO FOREWORD

A strong start. A lifelong foundation.

Support at the start. Belonging for life.

Dear Stakeholders, Partners, and Friends,

Since 2022, our responsibility has been to be there for every new Muslim beyond the shahada. The declaration of faith is a beautiful moment; building a new way of life is where the journey begins.

Our mission is to support every new Muslim’s well-being and early development. Our vision is an interconnected ecosystem of care across the West, helping people build confidence, consistency, and belonging.

This report shares our progress and future plans, shaped by four years of listening to our community.

Looking ahead

We are grateful to our founders, team, and stakeholders. Together, we are scaling our boxes, expanding local hubs, developing a helpline, and mapping community growth.

Thank you for helping us welcome, protect, and nurture our community’s future.

Houssem HassaniChief Executive Officer, New Muslim Centre

03 / A GIFT FOREVER

The New Muslim Box

A welcome that lasts.

This welcome gift provides practical resources at a defining moment. We have delivered 10,000 packs to new Muslims across the UK, USA, Canada, New Zealand, and Australia.

Each box offers direction, reassurance, and a connection to the wider Muslim community.

05 / A JOURNEY OF A LIFETIME

The Umrah Programme

Faith brought to life.

Launched in 2026, our first experiential development programme took new Muslims to Mecca and Medina.

By bringing the Qur’an and Seerah to life, and teaching wudu, salah, and du’a where faith began, the programme helped build a confident identity and lifelong resilience.

07 / A LIFELINE

The New Muslim Helpline

Support when it matters most.

We aim to launch a nationwide helpline in the second half of 2027, dedicated to the immediate and severe struggles faced by new Muslims.

We are securing the funding needed to provide swift, effective support for our most vulnerable brothers and sisters.

08 / DATA DRIVEN

Finding Every New Muslim

We cannot support who we cannot find.

We are developing the first system at scale to understand how many people are accepting Islam and where.

This research will help us measure the community’s growing needs. By sharing the data publicly, we aim to help organisations direct support where it is needed and build smarter solutions.

In developmentresearch & community mapping
